Hi! I'm a secon-years PhD student in University of Washington, advised by Prof.[Jenq-Neng Hwang](https://people.ece.uw.edu/hwang/) and [Linda Shapiro](https://people.ece.uw.edu/shapiro/index.html). Previously, I obtained my master's degree from Sun Yat-sen University, supervised by Prof.[Yao Lu](https://cse.sysu.edu.cn/teacher/Luyao).

My current research interest is in human-centered AI, including human motion representation and environment interaction. I am also working on medical image analysis across many modalities. I have extensive experience in image representation and data generation based on diffusion models.

I have a comprehensive background in academia and industry, and have worked as a machine learning engineer, research scientist, and software engineer at several well-known institutions. My professional experience includes developing segmentation and registration algorithms for CT and MRI images for UW Harborview Medical Center, and designing real-time medical image segmentation models for blood vessels at MicroPort MedBot. In addition, I have also worked at GE Healthcare China and Huiyan Medical Technology Co., Ltd., participating in the development of research tools and the construction of medical imaging cloud platforms.
