Hi! I'm a third-years PhD student in University of Washington, advised by Prof.[Jenq-Neng Hwang](https://people.ece.uw.edu/hwang/) and [Linda Shapiro](https://people.ece.uw.edu/shapiro/index.html). 

Previously, I obtained my master's degree from Sun Yat-sen University, supervised by Prof.[Yao Lu](https://cse.sysu.edu.cn/teacher/Luyao).

My current research interest is in human-centered AI, including human motion representation and environment interaction. I am also working on medical image analysis across different modalities. I have hands-on engineering experience as both a Machine Learning Engineer and a Software Engineer, with skills spanning from algorithm development to platform construction.
